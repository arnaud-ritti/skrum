# QRetro templates (52)

Source: https://qretro.com/templates

## 1. Went Well - To Improve - Action Items
`went-well-to-improve-action-items` · 3 columns

- **Went Well** — What worked and is worth repeating on purpose next sprint
- **To Improve** — Where the team lost momentum — problems, not people
- **Action Items** — Concrete steps with an owner and a date, not intentions

## 2. Start - Stop - Continue
`start-stop-continue` · 3 columns

- **Start** — New practices worth trying in the next cycle
- **Stop** — Habits that get in the way and should end now
- **Continue** — What already works and must survive the next change

## 3. 4Ls Retrospective
`4ls-liked-learned-lacked-longed-for` · 4 columns

- **Liked** — What you enjoyed or valued about the period
- **Learned** — What you know now that you did not know before
- **Lacked** — What was missing and made the work harder
- **Longed For** — What you wish existed, even if it is not ours to build

## 4. Sailboat Retrospective
`sailboat-exercise-sailboat-retrospective` · 4 columns

- **What is the wind pushing our sails that make us go fast** — What pushed us forward and we could repeat on purpose
- **What anchors are holding us back** — What slows us down and adds drag every sprint
- **What rocks are ahead of us that risk our future** — Risks ahead that will hurt us if nothing changes
- **What is our ideal island destination** — The goal we are sailing to — agree this before the rest

## 5. Mad Sad Glad
`mad-sad-glad` · 3 columns

- **😡 Mad** — What made you angry — the things that went wrong more than once
- **😟 Sad** — What was disappointing — expectations that were never met
- **😀 Glad** — What went well and is worth protecting next sprint

## 6. Thumbs up, Thumbs down, new ideas and recognition
`thumbs-up-thumbs-down-new-ideas-recognition` · 4 columns

- **👍Thumbs up** — What went well this sprint
- **👎Thumbs down** — What went wrong or felt frustrating
- **💡New Ideas** — Ideas worth trying, even half-formed ones
- **🏆Recognition** — Credit for people who made someone else's work easier

## 7. Lean Coffee
`lean-coffee` · 3 columns

- **To Discuss** — Proposed topics, ranked by votes
- **Discussing** — The single topic in play right now
- **Discussed** — Finished topics — check each one for a commitment

## 8. Original 4
`original-4-retrospective` · 4 columns

- **What went well?** — Successes worth naming out loud, however small
- **What didn’t go so well?** — Problems and misses — the facts, before the reasons
- **What have I learned?** — Insights you would pass to a teammate starting the same work
- **What still puzzles me?** — Open questions the team has not answered yet

## 9. Starfish Retrospective
`starfish-retrospective` · 5 columns

- **Keep Doing** — Working at the right level — protect it when we get busy
- **Less Of** — Valuable in smaller doses, not something to abolish
- **More of** — Already happens occasionally, deserves a bigger share of the week
- **Start Doing** — A new practice to try — one or two per sprint, no more
- **Stop Doing** — No remaining value at all

## 10. Three Little Pigs
`three-little-pigs-house-of-straws-house-of-sticks-house-of-bricks` · 3 columns

- **House of Straws** — Things that could easily fall apart
- **House of Sticks** — Things that are working but could be improved
- **House of Bricks** — Things that are stong and stable

## 11. Dot Voting
`dot-voting` · 1 columns

- **Topics | Ideas** — Every option under consideration — one card per option, then vote

## 12. Speed Car Retrospective
`speed-car-retrospective-engine-parachute` · 2 columns

- **Engine** — What make us move faster?
- **Parachute** — What is slowing us down?

## 13. Happy - Meh - Sad
`happy-meh-sad` · 3 columns

- **Happy :)** — Moments that gave the team energy
- **Meh :|** — Things that were neither good nor bad — and that is the signal
- **Sad :(** — What drained the team or went clearly wrong

## 14. Idea Prioritization
`ideas-low-medium-high-priority` · 3 columns

- **Low Priority** — Worth doing eventually, nothing breaks if it waits
- **Medium Priority** — Should happen in the next cycle or two
- **High Priority** — Costs the team or customers now — pick these up first

## 15. Harry Potter
`harry-potter-retrospective` · 5 columns

- **Felix Felicis** — Good things!
- **Baruffio's Brain Elixir** — Learned!
- **Petrificus Totalus** — What slowed us down?!
- **Triwizard Cup** — Shout outs!
- **Action Items** — Take things into consideration

## 16. KALM Retrospective
`keep-add-more-less` · 4 columns

- **Keep** — Works today and we would lose it first under pressure
- **Add** — Something we do not have yet and want to try
- **More** — Already happens occasionally and should happen routinely
- **Less** — Useful in smaller doses — reduce it rather than abolish it

## 17. Marie Kondo Method
`marie-kondo-joy-throw-out-recycle` · 3 columns

- **Brings You Joy** — What went well, happy to keep
- **Throw Out** — What went wrong, don't want to keep
- **Recycle** — What you want to improve and reuse

## 18. Game of Thrones
`game-of-thrones` · 4 columns

- **The castle** — (What are we great at?)
- **The hole** — (What are the current issues/problems?)
- **The wall** — (What could make us a better team? )
- **The White Walkers** — (What are the potential risks/challenges ?)

## 19. Love - Want - Hate - Learn
`i-love-i-want-to-i-hate-now-i-know-that` · 4 columns

- **I love ...** — What you genuinely enjoy about how the team works
- **I want to ...** — Changes you want, phrased as your own wish
- **I hate ...** — What frustrates you — the honest, unfiltered version
- **Now, I know that** — Discoveries from this sprint the whole team should share

## 20. Kudos Retrospective
`kudos-went-well-to-improve` · 4 columns

- **Kudos** — Thank a specific person for something specific
- **Went well** — What worked and should be protected next sprint
- **To improve** — Where the team lost time or quality
- **Action items** — Steps with an owner, taken out of the column above

## 21. Pros and Cons
`pros-and-cons` · 2 columns

- **Pros** — Arguments for the option — benefits, not hopes
- **Cons** — Costs, risks and what the option locks the team into

## 22. Sprint Diagnostics
`sprint-diagnostics-retrospective` · 4 columns

- **How was this Sprint? Rate 1-10 (communication, collaboration, quality, pressure?)** — A number plus one sentence on what drove it
- **BIG LIKE event/or Big Dislike;** — The single strongest positive or negative moment of the sprint
- **Is there anything we could do even better/differently?How?** — Improvements phrased as a change, not a complaint
- **Actions** — Owner and date for each change the team agreed on

## 23. 3 L's
`like-learned-lacked` · 3 columns

- **Like** — What you appreciated about the sprint
- **Learned** — New knowledge — about the product, the system or the team
- **Lacked** — What was missing: information, time, access or a decision

## 24. DAKI
`daki-drop-add-keep-improve` · 4 columns

- **Drop** — No remaining value — a report nobody reads, a step already automated
- **Add** — A new practice to try, one or two per sprint
- **Keep** — Works today and should be defended under pressure
- **Improve** — Nearly right — needs fixing rather than removing

## 25. SWOT Analysis
`swot-analysis` · 4 columns

- **Strength** — Internal advantages the team can rely on
- **Weakness** — Internal gaps that cost the team time or quality
- **Opportunity** — External openings worth taking while they are open
- **Threat** — External risks that would hurt if nothing changes

## 26. The Good - The Bad - The Ugly
`the-good-the-bad-the-ugly` · 3 columns

- **The Good** — What went well or worked well
- **The Bad** — What didn't go as planned or problems that surfaced
- **The Ugly** — Things that the project team couldn't change but should allow folks to call out

## 27. Agile Kanban
`kanban-todo-doing-done` · 3 columns

- **To Do** — Agreed work not started yet
- **Doing** — In progress right now, with a name attached
- **Done** — Finished and verified — proof the team moves

## 28. Six Thinking Hats
`six-thinking-hats` · 6 columns

- **Green Hat** — Creativity: alternatives and ideas, no judgement yet
- **Blue Hat** — Process: what this discussion is for and what stays out of it
- **White Hat** — Facts: numbers and observations, no interpretation
- **Red Hat** — Feelings: gut reactions, no justification required
- **Black Hat** — Caution: risks and what could go wrong
- **Yellow Hat** — Benefits: value and what already works

## 29. Post Mortem Retro
`post-mortem-retrospective` · 5 columns

- **What we liked** — What held up under pressure and should stay
- **What we missed** — Gaps the incident exposed: alerts, docs, access
- **What I learned** — What each person now knows that they did not before
- **For next time** — Follow-ups with an owner so the same failure is caught earlier
- **Appreciations** — Credit for people who carried the incident

## 30. Rose Bud Thorn
`rose-bud-thorn` · 3 columns

- **Rose** — What is clearly working right now
- **Bud** — Early signs worth investing in before they fade
- **Thorn** — What hurts and keeps hurting until someone fixes it

## 31. Hopes and Fears
`hopes-and-fears` · 2 columns

- **Hopes** — What the team wants this project or cycle to become
- **Fears** — What could go wrong — said out loud while it is still cheap

## 32. Good Bad Better Best
`good-bad-better-best` · 4 columns

- **Good** — Went well
- **Bad** — Went poorly
- **Better** — Improvement opportunities
- **Best** — Recognize outstanding results, performances, actions, people

## 33. Likes / Wishes / Wonders
`likes-wishes-wonders` · 3 columns

- **Likes** — What you appreciate about how the team works today
- **Wishes** — Changes you would like, phrased as a wish rather than a demand
- **Wonders** — Open questions and doubts nobody has answered yet

## 34. OKR Template
`okr-objective-key-result` · 3 columns

- **Key Results** — Measurable outcomes that prove the objective is met
- **Initiatives** — Work the team will actually do to move those numbers
- **Objectives** — Where the team wants to be — qualitative and ambitious

## 35. Retrospective Template: WWW
`www-worked-well-kinda-worked-didnt-worked` · 3 columns

- **Worked well** — Clear successes to keep deliberately
- **Kinda Worked** — Half-successes: the idea was right, the execution was not
- **Didn't Worked** — What failed and should not be repeated as is

## 36. Safety Check for Retrospective
`safety-check-retrospective` · 5 columns

- **5** — No problem — I will say anything, including uncomfortable things
- **4** — Mostly comfortable — I would soften how I phrase one or two topics
- **3** — Selective — I will talk about process, not about people or my own mistakes
- **2** — Guarded — I will agree with others but raise nothing myself
- **1** — Silent — I will say nothing meaningful in this session

## 37. Fishbone Analysis Retrospective Template
`fishbone-analysis` · 6 columns

- **People** — Causes rooted in skills, staffing or knowledge held by one person
- **Process** — Causes in how the team works: steps skipped, rules unwritten
- **Tools** — Causes in tooling: gaps in alerts, tests, environments
- **Program** — Causes in planning: scope, funding, deadlines
- **Environment** — Causes outside the team: vendors, traffic, org changes
- **Solutions** — Fixes that address the causes above, with an owner

## 38. WRAP Retrospective
`wrap-retrospective` · 4 columns

- **😇 What are your wishes?** — What you want for the team going forward
- **🤗 What do you appreciate?** — Credit for people whose work made yours easier
- **😨 What risks do you see?** — Risks you can see coming that nobody is handling yet
- **😵 What things are puzzling you?** — Things that do not add up and deserve a closer look

## 39. Learning Matrix
`learning-matrix` · 4 columns

- **:)** — What went well and is worth repeating
- **:(** — What went badly and cost the team
- **Idea!** — Proposals for the next sprint
- **Appreciation** — Thanks addressed to specific people

## 40. RAID Log Analysis
`raid-analysis-risks-assumptions-issues-dependencies` · 4 columns

- **Risks** — Things that may go wrong later — not yet real
- **Assumptions** — What the plan takes for granted and nobody verified
- **Issues** — Problems that are already happening and need an owner
- **Dependencies** — External work or decisions the team is waiting on

## 41. Liked - Lacked - Change Retrospective Template
`liked---lacked---change` · 4 columns

- **Liked** — What helped us move forward?
- **Lacked** — What held us back?
- **What can I change?** — What should I do differently?
- **What can we change?** — What should we do differently as a team?

## 42. Time: Added Stolen Restored
`time-added-stolen-restored` · 3 columns

- **Which of your plans/choices add more time to your day?** — Habits and choices that give you back hours
- **Which personal choices steal time from your day?** — Where your time leaks: interruptions, meetings, rework
- **How do/would you reclaim lost time to your day?** — Concrete ways to reclaim the hours listed above

## 47. Plus Delta Template
`plus-delta` · 2 columns

## 48. Energy Levels
`energy-levels` · 4 columns

- **Fully Charged** — What gave you energy and kept you going
- **Medium Power** — Steady but unremarkable — the sprint neither lifted nor drained you
- **Low Battery** — What exhausted you or drained your motivation
- **Sprint Energy** — How the team's energy moved across the sprint as a whole

## 49. Weather Forecast Retrospective
`weather-forecast-retrospective` · 4 columns

- **Stormy** — What has been tempestuous? Meaning: What issues have you been confronted with? In your opinion, what stooped team productivity to advance?
- **Rainy** — What has been troublesome? Meaning: What challenges did you face during the project? Were you able to overcome it? How? What did you do to solve the issues?
- **Cloudy** — What has been rosy? Meaning: What were the positive experiences you have had so far? Did things go well according to what initially was planned? Did you have support from your colleagues?
- **Sunny** — What has been bright? Meaning: Do you think that the sprint goals have been met? Do you feel positive and happy about your contribution to the team's outcome?

## 50. ESVP (Explorers, Shoppers, Vacationers, Prisoners)
`esvp-explorers-shoppers-vacationers-prisoners` · 4 columns

- **Explorers** — Want to discover new ideas and insights
- **Shoppers** — Analyse information and want to learn at least one useful idea during the realtor meeting
- **Vacationers** — Aren’t interested in retrospectives, but like being away from the daily grind
- **Prisoners** — Feel forced to attend the meeting and would prefer doing something else

## 51. SOAR Analysis
`soar-analysis` · 4 columns

- **Strenghts** — What do we excel at?
- **Opportunities** — What niches and opportunities we can utilize?
- **Aspirations** — What do we want to achieve?
- **Results** — How can we measure that the goals and aspirations were achieved?

## 52. Pre Mortem
`pre-mortem` · 4 columns

- **(Hypothetical) The project was a failure! What went wrong?** — Imagine the project already failed — describe how it happened
- **What didn’t we do?** — Steps the team skipped on the way to that failure
- **What current problems remain?** — Problems that exist today and would make the failure worse
- **Any other concerns?** — Anything else that nags at you and has no owner
