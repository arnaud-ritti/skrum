**How one page serves ScreenDashboard + ScreenTeam.** The app has one team page and no Sessions / Mood / Members page (spec §6.3). The page follows ScreenTeam (it is the one that matches the real props and features); ScreenDashboard contributes only what the back end can feed. Desktop (>= 64rem): header, creation tiles, then `grid-template-columns: minmax(0,1fr) 22.5rem` (`tm-grid`): main column = Retrospectives, Planning poker, Whiteboards; aside = Health check card, Members card. Below 64rem: one column, aside after the main sections. Below 40rem: tiles 2x2 then 1 column (`auto-fit minmax(14rem,1fr)`), poker table becomes a card list.
