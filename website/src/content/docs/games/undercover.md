Undercover is a game for **3 to 12 players**, in a game room or a retrospective icebreaker. Most players are civilians with the same secret word. The Undercover players receive a similar, different word. There is no Mr White role.

## Start a round

As the host, choose **Undercover** and set the **Number of Undercover players** before starting. The default is one for 3–6 players, two for 7–11 and three for 12. Civilians must remain the majority.

Each player can reveal their own word on their screen. They do not see their camp or anyone else's word. Players who join after the round starts watch until the next round.

## Clues, discussion and voting

1. Each surviving player gives a clue aloud, in the displayed order, without saying their word. The host advances to the next player.
2. After the clues, discuss who might have a different word. The host opens voting when the discussion is over.
3. Each survivor votes for another survivor. Votes are private and can be changed or withdrawn until the host closes voting. The host can close it once at least one vote has been cast.
4. The player with the most votes is eliminated and their camp is revealed. A tie starts another vote restricted to the tied players.

If neither camp has won, the survivors begin another cycle of clues, discussion and voting. Eliminated players can watch but cannot give clues or vote. There is no timer for individual turns.

## Winning and points

The civilians win when all Undercover players have been eliminated. The Undercover win when they are at least as numerous as the remaining civilians.

Every member of the winning camp receives **5 points**, including its eliminated players. The result reveals the words and camps. A round that is skipped, times out or is abandoned gives no winner and no points.
