## What it is

Three columns, none of them negative. Criticism is not excluded: it is rephrased, as a wish (what I would like instead) or as a wonder (a question nobody has answered).

## Goal

Feedback that people can hear: what to keep, constructive proposals, and the doubts that would otherwise stay unspoken.

## When to use it

- With a new team, newcomers or guests who do not yet dare to criticise.
- After a tense period, when the team needs to rebuild trust.
- To get feedback on something specific: a workshop, a project, a way of working.

## When to pick another format

When a real problem has to be named plainly, a wish can blur it: [Went well, To improve, Action ideas](../went-well-to-improve-actions/) or [Mad, Sad, Glad](../mad-sad-glad/) say it straight. To decide on practices, take [Start, Stop, Continue](../start-stop-continue/). If the team also needs to look at what is coming, [WRAP](../wrap/) adds a column for risks.

## How to run it

1. **Likes**: ask what each person appreciates about how the team works today.
2. **Wishes**: ask for changes, phrased as a wish and not as a demand. Starting the card with "I wish we" helps.
3. **Wonders**: ask for open questions and doubts. Starting the card with "I wonder why" or "I wonder whether" helps.

Take **Wonders** first in the discussion. Answer what can be answered on the spot; what cannot becomes one person's task to find out and report. Then group and vote on **Wishes**. For the most voted, ask what would make the wish come true, and write the action with an owner and a date. A wish can also be declined: saying why, openly, is better than leaving it on the board. End with **Likes**.
