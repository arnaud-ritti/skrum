## What it is

Five columns numbered from 5 to 1, each describing how freely someone is ready to speak in this session. Each person places one card in the column that matches their state. It is a check-in, not a retrospective: it does not look at the sprint.

## Goal

The facilitator and the team know how much honesty the session can carry before it starts, and adapt it to that instead of finding out through silence.

## When to use it

With a new team, or one you do not know yet. After a conflict, an incident that ended in blame, a reorganisation or a change of manager. When retrospectives have become polite and nothing difficult is raised. Run it as a short session of its own, just before the retrospective it prepares.

## When to pick another format

It only works anonymously, and anonymity needs numbers: in a very small group, people can guess who placed a low card, so talk to them one by one instead. To learn why people came, more than how safe they feel, use [ESVP](../esvp/). For the retrospective that follows a mixed reading, [Happy, Meh, Sad](../happy-meh-sad/) asks about moments, not about people.

## How to run it

Turn on **Anonymous cards** when you [create the retro](../../create-a-retro/), and say so before anyone writes. Read the five descriptions aloud, then ask each person for one card, with a word or a line on what would move them one column up.

- **5**: nothing is held back.
- **4**: one or two topics are softened.
- **3**: process only, not people or one's own mistakes.
- **2**: agreement with others, and nothing raised.
- **1**: nothing meaningful is said.

There is nothing to group or to vote on. Count the cards in each column aloud, and never ask who placed a card. Mostly 5 and 4: go on with the retrospective you planned. Several cards at 3: keep to process topics. Cards at 2 or 1: set the plan aside, read what the cards say would help, and take one action from it.
