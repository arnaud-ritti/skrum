## What it is

A decision aid for a single option. The team states the proposal in one sentence, then lists the arguments for it and the arguments against it in two columns before it decides.

## Goal

A decision the team can explain, taken with the arguments of both sides in front of everyone. Failing that, a clear idea of what is missing in order to decide.

## When to use it

- One proposal is on the table: adopt a tool, change the length of the sprint, split a team, rewrite a component.
- The discussion has gone round several times and the same people repeat the same arguments.
- A few voices dominate. Writing first gives the quieter members the same space.
- Before committing to something that will be hard to undo.

## When to pick another format

Two columns hold one option. With several candidates, narrow them down with [Dot Voting](../dot-voting/) and weigh the finalists one at a time. When the question depends on what happens outside the team as much as inside it, [SWOT Analysis](../swot/) keeps the two apart. When the decision is taken and the worry is how it could go wrong, run a [Pre-mortem](../pre-mortem/).

## How to run it

Write the option as one sentence that everyone accepts as a fair description, and say what not doing it means, since that is the real alternative.

1. **Pros**: ask for benefits, not hopes. What would be better, for whom, and how would the team notice? One argument per card. Ask everyone to write here, the sceptics included.
2. **Cons**: ask for the costs, the risks, and what the option locks the team into: what becomes harder to change afterwards? Ask the supporters to write here too.

Merge the duplicates, then vote for the arguments that weigh most, on either side: the vote is on arguments, not on the option. The number of cards proves nothing, since one heavy objection can outweigh a column of small advantages.

Discuss the leading arguments of each column, then decide: yes, no, or yes on a condition. Record the decision as an action with an owner. If the group cannot decide, name the information that is missing and who will get it.
