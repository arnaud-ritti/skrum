## What it is

Four columns. Two look back: what helped us move forward, what held us back. Two ask for change at two levels: what can I change, what can we change. Splitting the personal from the collective is what marks the format.

## Goal

Two kinds of commitment: what each person will do differently on their own authority, and what the team changes together.

## When to use it

- When the actions of your retrospectives always begin with "the team should" and nobody owns them.
- When the members are at ease enough to look at their own part.
- When much of what holds the team back is within individual reach: how people prepare, review, ask for help.

## When to pick another format

**What can I change?** asks for self-criticism in front of others. Where trust is low, run a [Safety Check](../safety-check/) first, or take [Start, Stop, Continue](../start-stop-continue/), which stays at the level of the team. If the obstacles lie outside the team's control, [The Good, the Bad and the Ugly](../good-bad-ugly/) has a column for them.

## How to run it

1. **Liked**: ask what helped the team move forward.
2. **Lacked**: ask what held it back.
3. **What can I change?** Ask each person what they should do differently. People write for themselves only: never a card about what a colleague should change.
4. **What can we change?** Ask what the team should do differently as a team.

Group and vote on **Lacked** and on **What can we change?**, discuss the most voted cards, and agree on team actions with an owner and a date. The personal column is not voted on. Invite each person to read one of their cards aloud as a commitment; nobody comments. At the next retrospective, begin by asking how those commitments went.
