## What it is

A scale of three steps applied to what the team tried. The middle column is the point of the format: **Kinda worked** holds the half-successes, where the idea was right and the execution was not.

## Goal

To stop good ideas from being thrown out with a poor first attempt. The team decides what to keep, what to try again differently and what to abandon.

## When to use it

- After a sprint of experiments: a new practice, a new tool, a trial of a process.
- To review the actions decided at the previous retrospective.
- For a short session: three columns and brief cards.
- With a team that judges in black and white.

## When to pick another format

The format evaluates what was done. It does not ask for new ideas, and it does not ask how people feel. For new practices, take [Start, Stop, Continue](../start-stop-continue/); for feelings, [Mad, Sad, Glad](../mad-sad-glad/). If what sits between good and bad in your sprint is something young and promising, not something half done, [Rose, Bud, Thorn](../rose-bud-thorn/) has a column for it.

## How to run it

1. **Worked well**: ask for clear successes, the ones to keep deliberately.
2. **Kinda worked**: ask for half-successes, and have each card say which half worked.
3. **Didn't work**: ask what failed and should not be repeated as is. The words "as is" matter: the aim behind a failed attempt may still be worth pursuing.

Group and vote, then spend most of the discussion on **Kinda worked**. For each of the most voted cards, decide what to change in the execution for a second attempt: that is the action, with an owner and a date. For **Didn't work**, decide between giving up the aim and reaching it another way. Close with **Worked well** and say aloud what the team keeps.
