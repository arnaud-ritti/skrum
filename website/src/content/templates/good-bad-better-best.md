## What it is

Four columns that climb: good, bad, better, best. The first two review the sprint. **Better** collects improvement opportunities. **Best** is reserved for what stood out: outstanding results, performances, actions and people.

## Goal

Improvement actions, and an explicit record of the best of the sprint, so that excellent work is named, understood and can happen again.

## When to use it

- At the end of a successful sprint or release, to learn from what went right.
- When the team's retrospectives dwell on problems.
- When individual contributions go unnoticed.

## When to pick another format

After a sprint that went badly, a column for the best can ring hollow: [Mad, Sad, Glad](../mad-sad-glad/) or [The Good, the Bad and the Ugly](../good-bad-ugly/) fit better. If thanking each other is the main purpose, [Kudos](../kudos/) opens with it.

## How to run it

Before the writing, say how **Good** and **Best** differ: the first is what met expectations, the second what went beyond them.

1. **Good**: ask what went well.
2. **Bad**: ask what went poorly.
3. **Better**: ask for improvement opportunities. A card may answer a problem of the previous column, or make something good better still.
4. **Best**: ask for outstanding results, performances, actions and people. Names are welcome, with what the person did.

Open the discussion with **Best**, and for each card ask what made it possible. The answer is often a practice worth making routine, and that is an action. Then group and vote on **Bad** and **Better**, read them side by side, and agree on the improvements to carry out, each with an owner and a date.
