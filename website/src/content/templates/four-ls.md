## What it is

Four columns, each a word starting with L. Two are about what was there: what people liked and what they learned. Two are about what was not. The difference between those two matters: **Lacked** is what was missing and made the work harder, **Longed for** is what you wish existed, even if it is not the team's to build.

## Goal

A shared picture of the period that includes what the team learned, not only what went well or badly. The gaps come out sorted: those the team can close itself, and those it has to ask someone else for.

## When to use it

- At the end of a longer period: a release, a project, a quarter.
- After work on something new, where learning was a large part of the effort.
- With a team that tends to list only problems: two of the four columns are positive.

## When to pick another format

In a regular sprint the fourth column often stays empty: [3 Ls](../three-ls/) drops it. If the team wants to decide on changes of behaviour directly, take [Start, Stop, Continue](../start-stop-continue/). The format touches feelings only indirectly; when frustration is high, [Mad, Sad, Glad](../mad-sad-glad/) lets people say it.

## How to run it

1. **Liked**: ask what each person enjoyed or valued about the period.
2. **Learned**: ask what people know now that they did not know before, about the product, the tools or the team itself.
3. **Lacked**: ask what was missing and made the work harder: information, time, a skill, a decision.
4. **Longed for**: ask what people wish existed, including things outside the team's reach.

Group the cards and vote. Cards of **Lacked** usually turn into actions the team owns. Cards of **Longed for** often need someone outside the team: the action is then for one member to carry the request to the right person. Before closing, read **Learned** aloud and decide what deserves to be written down or shared with other teams.
