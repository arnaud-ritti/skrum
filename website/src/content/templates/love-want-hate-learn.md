## What it is

Each column is the beginning of a sentence that the card finishes: I love, I want to, I hate, now I know that. Everything is said in the first person, and the words are strong on purpose.

## Goal

Honest, personal statements in place of general remarks. The team learns what each member cares about, wants and cannot stand, and shares what the sprint taught.

## When to use it

- When the cards have become impersonal, of the kind "communication could be better".
- With a team whose members know each other well and can take a blunt sentence.
- When you want individual wishes heard, not only the team's problems.

## When to pick another format

**I hate…** invites the unfiltered version. In a team with little trust or an open conflict, it can hurt more than it helps: [Likes, Wishes, Wonders](../likes-wishes-wonders/) asks for similar things in a softer voice, and a [Safety Check](../safety-check/) tells you beforehand how freely people will speak.

## How to run it

Ask everyone to write full sentences that start with the words of the column.

1. **I love…** What each person genuinely enjoys about how the team works.
2. **I want to…** A change the person wants, phrased as their own wish: "I want to pair more often", not "we should pair".
3. **I hate…** What frustrates them, in the honest version. One rule: a card is about a situation or a practice, never about a person.
4. **Now I know that…** A discovery from this sprint that the whole team should share.

Group and vote. Start the discussion with **I hate…** and **I want to…** together: a frustration and a wish are often two sides of one subject, and the wish is already half the solution. Turn the most voted into actions with an owner and a date. Close by reading **Now I know that…** aloud, and end on **I love…**
