## What it is

Four questions whose subjects spell the name: wishes, risks, appreciations, puzzles. On the board the columns come in another order: wishes, appreciations, risks, puzzles. None of them reviews the sprint as such; the format asks where the team stands and what is coming.

## Goal

An early warning. The team sees the risks nobody is handling and the things that do not add up, beside what its members want and whom they thank.

## When to use it

- In the middle of a project or before a milestone, when looking ahead matters more than reviewing.
- When the team senses trouble and cannot name it yet.
- In turn with a format that looks back, to break the routine.

## When to pick another format

WRAP does not go over what happened. After an eventful sprint, take [Went well, To improve, Action ideas](../went-well-to-improve-actions/) or [Original 4](../original-four/), which keeps a column for puzzles. At the very start of a project, [Hopes and Fears](../hopes-and-fears/) is simpler. When risks dominate, the [RAID Log](../raid/) or a [Pre-mortem](../pre-mortem/) go further.

## How to run it

1. **😇 What are your wishes?** Ask what each person wants for the team going forward.
2. **🤗 What do you appreciate?** Ask for credit to people whose work made someone else's easier, with names and specifics.
3. **😨 What risks do you see?** Ask for risks people can see coming that nobody is handling yet.
4. **😵 What is puzzling you?** Ask for things that do not add up and deserve a closer look.

Read the appreciations aloud first. Then group and vote on the risks: for each of the most voted, decide who watches it or what reduces it, with an owner and a date. Take the puzzles next: some are answered on the spot, the others are given to one person to look into. End with the wishes, and pick those the team can make true soon.
