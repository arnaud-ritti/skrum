## What it is

Three columns about behaviour, not about events. The format does not ask how the sprint went: it asks what the team should begin doing, what it should end, and what it should keep. Every card is already half an action.

## Goal

A small set of changes to how the team works, agreed by everyone, and an explicit list of the practices those changes must not break.

## When to use it

- When the team wants decisions and is tired of describing problems.
- After a change of process, tools or people, to sort what the change should keep.
- When time is short: the cards are brief and close to actions.
- With a team that already knows its problems well.

## When to pick another format

The format is binary: a practice starts or stops. When the honest answer is "a little more" or "a little less", use [Starfish](../starfish/) or [KALM](../kalm/). It also leaves no room for an account of what happened, or for feelings. After an eventful sprint, start from [Went well, To improve, Action ideas](../went-well-to-improve-actions/) or from [Mad, Sad, Glad](../mad-sad-glad/).

## How to run it

1. **Start**: ask for new practices worth trying in the next cycle. One practice per card, written as something the team would do, not as a wish.
2. **Stop**: ask for habits that get in the way and should end now. This is the hardest column to fill. Say aloud that stopping a practice is no judgement of whoever introduced it.
3. **Continue**: ask what already works and must survive the next change. Do not skip it: it is the list you check new ideas against.

Group similar cards, vote across the three columns, then discuss the most voted. A card of **Start** or **Stop** becomes an action once it has an owner and a date. Limit what the team starts at once, and check each new practice against **Continue** before you agree on it.
