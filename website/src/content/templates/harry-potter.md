## What it is

A full retrospective under names borrowed from the Harry Potter stories. A luck potion stands for the good things, a brain elixir for what the team learned, a spell that freezes its target for what slowed the team down, and a tournament trophy for the shout-outs. The fifth column says plainly what it is: action items.

## Goal

What any complete retro gives: what to keep, what was learned, what blocked, who deserves thanks and what to do next. The theme changes only the mood in which the team gets there.

## When to use it

Take it with a team that knows the stories and enjoys them, when the usual board has become routine and a change of scenery would wake the session up. The column of shout-outs makes it a good choice after a demanding stretch.

## When to pick another format

A theme works only when everyone is in on it. If someone has never read the books, the titles are riddles: [Kudos](../kudos/) covers much the same ground in plain words. After an incident the playful dress can jar, and a [Post-mortem](../post-mortem/) has close to the same columns in a sober tone.

## How to run it

Read the descriptions aloud once, since the titles do not explain themselves.

1. **Felix Felicis**: ask for the good things, whether they came from luck or from skill.
2. **Baruffio's Brain Elixir**: ask what the team learned, about the product, the tools or each other.
3. **Petrificus Totalus**: ask what slowed the team down or froze it in place: a blocker, a wait, a decision that never came.
4. **Triwizard Cup**: ask for shout-outs. A good one names a person and what they did.
5. **Action items**: ask for things to take into consideration. Ideas can land here from the start.

Read the shout-outs aloud, then group and vote on the cards under Petrificus Totalus and discuss the first ones. Finish in the last column: keep the items the team commits to, and give each an owner.
