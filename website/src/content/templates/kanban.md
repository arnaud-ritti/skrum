## What it is

Three columns that show where a set of work stands: agreed but not started, in progress, finished. Used as a session, the board is a snapshot: everyone puts what they know on it, and the team reads the result together.

## Goal

An honest, shared picture of the work: what is really finished, what is in progress and who holds it, what was agreed and never touched. Then a decision about what is stuck.

## When to use it

- To review the actions agreed in earlier retrospectives before adding new ones.
- In the middle of a release or a migration, to check where it stands.
- When the team feels busy and cannot say what it has finished.

## When to pick another format

This board looks at the work and has no column for how the team works together: for that, take a retrospective such as [Start, Stop, Continue](../start-stop-continue/). If the list of things to do is not agreed yet, sort it first with [Idea Prioritization](../idea-prioritization/). If the work waits on people outside the team, [RAID Log](../raid/) has columns for risks and dependencies. The board is filled during a session and does not replace the place where the team follows its work day by day.

## How to run it

Name the scope first: the actions of the last retrospective, this release. Ask everyone to write one card per piece of work, in the column that matches its state today. Correct the placement during [grouping](../../grouping/), while a card can still be dragged from one column to another.

1. **To do**: agreed, not started. For each card, ask whether it is still agreed, and drop on purpose the old items nobody defends.
2. **Doing**: every card needs a name, the person working on it right now. A card without one goes back to **To do**. If the column holds more than the team can work on at once, ask what to finish before anything new begins.
3. **Done**: finished and verified. Ask how the team knows; what is almost done belongs in **Doing**. Read this column aloud: it is the proof that the team moves.

For each card stuck in **Doing**, agree on the next step, an owner and a date.
