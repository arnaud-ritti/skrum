## What it is

A sprint review with a column of thanks in front of it. **Kudos** is for thanking a specific person for something specific. **Went well** and **To improve** look back at the sprint, and **Action items** holds the steps the team draws from **To improve**.

## Goal

People hear, in front of the team, what their work meant to their colleagues, and the team leaves with a few steps that each have an owner.

## When to use it

After a sprint in which people carried each other: a release, an incident, someone covering for an absence. When good work goes unnoticed because the team only ever talks about problems. It also suits a team that is not used to thanking out loud: a column makes thanks part of the routine.

## When to pick another format

Thanks cannot be forced. In a team in open conflict the Kudos column fills with polite cards or stays empty: start with [Mad, Sad, Glad](../mad-sad-glad/), or read the room with a [Safety Check](../safety-check/) first. To end on appreciation instead of opening with it, take [Appreciation Retro](../appreciation/). If recognition already happens elsewhere, [Went well, To improve, Action ideas](../went-well-to-improve-actions/) is the same review in three columns.

## How to run it

1. **Kudos.** Ask who helped you this sprint, and with what exactly. One person and one fact per card, not "thanks, everyone".
2. **Went well.** Ask what worked and should be protected next sprint: what the team did, not only what turned out well.
3. **To improve.** Ask where the team lost time or quality. One problem per card.
4. **Action items.** Ask people to write the step they already have in mind for a problem they raised.

Once everyone has written, read the **Kudos** column aloud before anything else. Then group the cards, spend the votes on **To improve**, and discuss the top topics beside the steps proposed in **Action items**. Record the steps the team commits to as action items, each with an owner and a due date.
