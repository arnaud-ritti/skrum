## What it is

Four labels that could hardly be shorter: a smiling face, a frowning face, "Idea!" and "Appreciation". Think of a square cut into four quadrants; on the board they stand side by side as four columns.

## Goal

What the team takes from the sprint, in four kinds: what to repeat, what cost it, what to try, and whom to thank.

## When to use it

- For a short retrospective: the labels are read in a second and the cards stay brief.
- With a large or mixed group.
- For a regular sprint, as a change from columns with long titles.

## When to pick another format

The labels do not guide much. If the team needs fuller prompts, [Thumbs up, Thumbs down, New ideas, Recognition](../thumbs-up-down-ideas-recognition/) asks the same four questions in words. If one problem needs depth, use [Fishbone Analysis](../fishbone/). If the mood is the subject, [Mad, Sad, Glad](../mad-sad-glad/) gives feelings three columns, not one face.

## How to run it

Since the faces say little, tell the group what each column is for before anyone writes.

1. **:)** What went well and is worth repeating.
2. **:(** What went badly and cost the team something: time, quality, calm.
3. **Idea!** Proposals for the next sprint. An idea may answer a problem or come from nowhere.
4. **Appreciation**: thanks addressed to specific people, with what they did.

Read the last column aloud first; it needs no vote. Group and vote on the other three. Discuss the most voted cards of **:(** together with the cards of **Idea!** and keep the proposals the team wants to carry out, each with an owner and a date. Before closing, look at **:)** once more and name what is worth doing again on purpose.
