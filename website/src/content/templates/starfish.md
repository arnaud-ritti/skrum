## What it is

Five columns, one per arm of the starfish, all about the team's practices. Between keeping a practice and stopping it, the format offers two intermediate answers, less of it and more of it, and a place for what is new.

## Goal

A calibrated view of how the team works: not only what to add or remove, but the right dose of what it already does.

## When to use it

- With an established team that has many practices in place.
- When [Start, Stop, Continue](../start-stop-continue/) feels blunt.
- When discussions polarise, for example around a meeting some want to abolish: "less of" gives a third answer.
- When you have a full session: five columns take time to write and to read.

## When to pick another format

For a short retrospective or a team that is just starting, three columns are enough: [Start, Stop, Continue](../start-stop-continue/). Starfish asks about habits, not events: after a sprint with something to digest, take [Went well, To improve, Action ideas](../went-well-to-improve-actions/) or [Mad, Sad, Glad](../mad-sad-glad/).

## How to run it

Before the writing, explain the two pairs that are easy to confuse: **Less of** against **Stop doing**, and **More of** against **Start doing**.

1. **Keep doing**: what works at the right level and must be protected when the team gets busy.
2. **Less of**: what is valuable in smaller doses, not something to abolish.
3. **More of**: what already happens occasionally and deserves a bigger share of the week.
4. **Start doing**: a new practice to try. The column asks for one or two per sprint, no more.
5. **Stop doing**: what has no remaining value at all. An empty column is a fine answer.

Two people may put the same practice in two different columns: that disagreement is the discussion to have. Group, vote and discuss the most voted cards. An action from **More of** or **Less of** needs a measure the team can check, and every action needs an owner and a date.
